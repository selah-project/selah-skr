## The Hebrew text

The underlying Hebrew text (OSHB / WLC 4.20) is in the public domain.
This license applies **only** to the rendering — the flows, the glosses,
the marks and the revision history — which are the Selah project's own
work.

## Plainly

This rendering is a **witness**, not the last word. It was produced by
machine, following a written discipline, and it is published as the Selah
project's own work — not as an ancient manuscript, and not as the work of
a scholarly committee. No native Saraiki reader has yet read it. Whoever
redistributes it should redistribute it knowing that.

Read it alongside the Hebrew.
